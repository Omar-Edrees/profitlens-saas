Actual ROAS ?Actual return on ad spend.
= (Selling price + customer shipping) × delivered orders ÷ ad budget

Revenue ÷ ad spend
Break-even CPA ?Max CPA you can pay and still cover all business costs (product + shipping + ads + salary).
= (Profit/unit − Salary/unit) × Conf% × Del%
If Actual CPA exceeds this → you are losing on the whole business.
—
Full business break-even
Break-even ROAS ?Minimum ROAS needed to cover all business costs.
= (Price + ShipC) × Conf% × Del% ÷ Break-even CPA
If Actual ROAS is below this → business is losing.
