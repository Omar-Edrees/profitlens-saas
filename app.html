🚚 Shipping zones ?Split your orders across 5 delivery zones. For each zone enter the shipping cost you pay (your cost), the shipping you charge the customer, and the % share of your orders that go there. The tool computes weighted-average values (weighted by order share) and uses them as your "Avg. shipping cost (your cost)" and "Shipping charged to customer". When unlocked, those two basic fields up in the Shipping section are locked and driven by these zones. Shares don't have to sum to exactly 100% — the averages are normalised by the total you enter.
