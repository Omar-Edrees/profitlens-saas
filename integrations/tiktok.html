Integration

TikTok Ads integration

ProfitLens uses the TikTok API for Business (Marketing API) to read advertising reports from TikTok Ads accounts whose owners authorize ProfitLens. The integration is read-only.

Why we use it

To calculate the real profit of a product, ProfitLens needs the ad spend and results for that product. Instead of typing these numbers in by hand every day, an advertiser can let ProfitLens read them from TikTok Ads. ProfitLens then puts the spend next to the store's orders and costs to show cost per order, ROAS and profit for each product.

Data we read

DataWhy we need it
Ad account (advertiser) ID, name, currency and time zoneTo show each account separately and use the right currency and dates.
Campaign, ad group and ad IDs and namesTo group results the same way the advertiser sees them in TikTok Ads Manager.
Landing page URL of each adTo match each ad to the product it promotes.
Daily reporting metrics: spend, impressions, clicks, conversions (such as complete payment) and conversion valueTo calculate cost per order, ROAS and profit per product.

We do not read or store any personal data of TikTok users, audiences, comments or videos.

What ProfitLens never does

  • It never creates, edits, pauses or deletes campaigns, ad groups, ads, audiences or creatives.
  • It never accesses ad accounts that were not authorized by their owner.
  • It never sells, rents or shares TikTok data with third parties, and never uses it for advertising or profiling.

How an advertiser connects

  • The advertiser contacts us to connect their TikTok Ads account.
  • We send TikTok's official authorization link. The advertiser signs in on TikTok, reviews the requested read-only permissions and approves them.
  • TikTok returns an authorization to ProfitLens. From then on ProfitLens reads the daily reports of the approved ad accounts only.

Permissions requested

  • Ad Account Management: read ad account information.
  • Ads Management: read campaigns, ad groups and ads (no create, update or delete).
  • Reporting: read performance reports.

Storage and security

Access tokens are kept on our servers only, are never shown in the browser, and are used only to read reports for the account that granted them. Reporting data is stored in the advertiser's private ProfitLens workspace, protected by sign-in, and visible only to that advertiser's team.

Disconnecting and deleting data

An advertiser can stop access at any time, either by asking us at support@profitlens.site or by removing ProfitLens from the authorized apps of their TikTok For Business account. When access is revoked we stop reading data immediately, delete the access token, and delete the stored TikTok reporting data within 30 days, or sooner on request.
