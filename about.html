About ProfitLens

ProfitLens is a profit analytics web app for e-commerce businesses, built and operated in Egypt. It helps store owners, media buyers and affiliate marketers understand the real profit of every product after all costs: product cost, shipping, returns, fees and advertising.

What we do

Users enter their product and cost numbers, or import advertising results from an ad account they connect with read-only access. ProfitLens calculates net profit, margin, real cost per order, ROAS and break-even ROAS, and shows which products can be scaled safely and which should be stopped.

Who uses ProfitLens

  • Online store owners who run their own ads or work with a media buyer.
  • Media buyers who manage ad budgets for e-commerce brands.
  • Affiliate marketers who test and price products.

How we handle data

Each user's data is private to their account. We use advertising platform data only to show reports to the advertiser who connected it, we never change anything in an ad account, and we never sell data. Details are in our Privacy Policy and on the TikTok Ads integration page.
