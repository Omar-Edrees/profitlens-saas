Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information ProfitLens ("we", "us") collects when you use profitlens.site and the ProfitLens web app, how we use it, and the choices you have. ProfitLens is operated from Egypt. Contact: support@profitlens.site.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), or your name and email address if you sign in with Google.
  • Data you enter: product names, selling prices, product costs, shipping and return costs, ad spend and other numbers you type into the app, plus any custom formula requests you send us.
  • Advertising platform data (optional): if you connect a TikTok Ads account, we receive the reporting data described in section 4.
  • Payment information: we record your plan and payment status. We do not store card numbers.
  • Support messages: messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Usage and device data: on our public website we use Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel to measure visits and the results of our own advertising. These services may set cookies and collect your IP address, browser type and pages visited.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and provide the app's calculations and reports.
  • To process payments, refunds and support requests.
  • To improve the app and measure our own marketing.
  • To send service messages, such as account and security notices.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your business data or advertising data to advertise to anyone.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that run the app for us, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Vercel: website and app hosting.
  • Google: Google sign-in (if you use it) and website analytics.
  • Meta and TikTok: advertising pixels on our public website only.

We may also disclose information if required by law. These providers may store data outside Egypt.

4. TikTok Ads data

If you authorize ProfitLens to access your TikTok Ads account through the TikTok API for Business:

  • We read only: ad account ID, name, currency and time zone; campaign, ad group and ad IDs and names; ad landing page URLs; and reporting metrics such as spend, impressions, clicks, conversions and conversion value.
  • We use this data only to show reports and profit calculations to you and the team members of your ProfitLens account.
  • We never create, change or delete anything in your ad account.
  • We do not access personal data of TikTok users, and we do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties or use it for advertising or profiling.
  • Access tokens are stored on our servers only and are never shown in the browser.
  • You can revoke access at any time by contacting us or by removing ProfitLens from the authorized apps in your TikTok For Business account. We then delete the access token immediately and the stored TikTok data within 30 days.

More details are on the TikTok Ads integration page.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and the data you enter while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as payment records. Advertising platform data is kept while the connection is active and deleted as described above.

6. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to disconnect an ad account, by emailing support@profitlens.site from your account's email address. We answer within 30 days. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Data is protected by sign-in and database access rules so each account can read only its own data, and access by our team is limited to what is needed to provide support. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

ProfitLens is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for important changes we will notify account holders by email.
