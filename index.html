Profit analytics for e-commerce stores

Know your real profit
before you spend more on ads.

ProfitLens is a web app for online store owners, media buyers and affiliate marketers. It turns your selling price, product cost, shipping, returns and ad spend into net profit, real cost per order, ROAS and break-even ROAS, and gives a clear Scale or Stop decision for every product.

The problem

Sales go up, but the profit is not there. Hidden costs eat the margin while the ad budget keeps growing.

Profit by guesswork

Price minus product cost is not profit. Shipping, returns, fees and ads all come out of the margin.

Scaling a losing product

An ad can look good while the product is below break-even, so every extra pound of budget loses more.

Slow decisions

Numbers spread over many spreadsheets delay every decision, and in advertising a delay costs money.

How it works

1

Add your numbers

Selling price, product cost, shipping and ad spend. Type them in, or import ad spend and results from an ad account you connect with read-only access.

2

ProfitLens calculates

Net profit, margin, real cost per order, ROAS and break-even ROAS, for each product.

3

Decide with numbers

See which product to scale, which to stop, and what it takes to reach the profit you want.

Features

Full profitability analysis

Net profit and real margin after every hidden cost, not only price minus cost.

Ad performance analysis

Real cost per order, ROAS and break-even ROAS for every product.

Scale detector

Shows which products are ready to scale safely and which are still below break-even.

Target profit simulator

Pick the profit you want; ProfitLens tells you the price and ad budget needed to reach it.

Product comparison

Compare products side by side to see which ones really make money.

Shipping and returns impact

See how shipping cost and returns change the profit of each product.

Ad platform reporting

Advertisers can connect their own TikTok Ads account so ProfitLens reads the spend and results of their campaigns, instead of typing them in. The connection is read-only and is approved by the advertiser on TikTok's own authorization page.

What ProfitLens reads

  • Spend, impressions, clicks and conversions per day
  • Campaign, ad group and ad names and IDs
  • The landing page URL of each ad, to match it to a product

What ProfitLens never does

  • Create, edit, pause or delete any campaign, ad group or ad
  • Access the personal data of TikTok users
  • Sell or share advertiser data with anyone
